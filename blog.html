Biggest AI Risk: Autonomous AI

We are standing at a critical juncture in human history. The technological marvels of AI are undeniable, but the eventual risks of runaway AI and self-replication scenarios are no longer just science fiction.

This post asks what happens when capability becomes autonomy—and argues for strict oversight, controlled deployment, and one non-negotiable safeguard: an off switch for every AI.

Before we push the boundaries of artificial intelligence any further, we need an off switch for every AI.
