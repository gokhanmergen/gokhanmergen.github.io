Reading List: Economics, Markets & Mind

Nearly every book I read or listened to over a few years, sent to a friend: economics, liberty, macro, risk, behavioral finance, investing, physics, philosophy and health.

Gökhan Mergen My Blog: Post AGI
Oct 1, 2017 · 12 min read Türkçe oku

Hi Friend,

We were discussing books last time. I definitely recommend listening to Thinking, Fast and Slow & other books that deal with psychological biases (relevant to model building and trading). I'd also highly recommend Nassim Taleb's books, like we discussed.

Below is a nearly complete set of books I've read or listened to in the last few years. There are books about stock investing, market bubbles, economics, macro investing, uncertainty in the real world. Each book tends to be 80-90% repetition of others, and 10-20% completely new. From that perspective, all books here have something interesting, and I'd recommend all of them. Though checking the reviews on Amazon is probably a more fool-proof idea :)

As you can see, I'm pretty much addicted to reading recently. In reality, I feel that I'm just trying to catch up with all the years where I read mostly math & science, and nothing else. Theorizing about the real world feels more fascinating to me than theorizing about wireless networks these days :)

Hope you'll find the list interesting or useful. Please let me know if you want to discuss any of them.

Cheers,

Gokhan
